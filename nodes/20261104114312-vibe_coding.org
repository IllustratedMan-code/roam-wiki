#+title: vibe-coding

Vibe coding is coding entirely through the use of AI agents,
e.g. claude code.

* Pitfalls
Vibe coding has the obvious problem of losing the direct connection
between programmer and program. It emulates the relationship between a
supervisor and a junior programmer. Because of this, the LLM can lie,
or obfuscate what code is being written, unintentionally or not.


** Code complexity
LLMs know more than you, but their critical thinking skills are
low. Meaning, they may implement features in unintuitive ways, but may
use methods that are unfamiliar or "shiny" that look impressive. It is
the programmers job to insist upon simplicity. Fancy optimizations are
important, but not at the cost of readability and more importantly
maintainability. It is important to limit the scope of any singular
change by the agent.
